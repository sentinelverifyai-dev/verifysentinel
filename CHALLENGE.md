# Prove it wrong

Sentinel says whether a picture was likely made by AI. When it can't tell, it says so instead of guessing. We
think that when it does answer, it is hard to fool. **Prove us wrong.**

## How to play

1. Take a picture whose origin you can prove. That means either one you made with an AI image generator, or a
   real photo you took yourself.
2. Check it with the free check, at [verifysentinel.com](https://verifysentinel.com) or in the
   [Hugging Face demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/verifysentinel/ai-image-detector). Both give you a
   **receipt link**.
3. Keep the exact file you checked, and the receipt link.

## What counts as a win

A **confident wrong answer**. All three of these must be true:
- Sentinel answered **"Likely made by AI"** for a real photo, or **"Likely a real photo"** for a picture made by
  AI.
- You have the **receipt link** for that exact file. Anyone can open it and confirm the file matches.
- You can show **where the picture came from**:
  - for an AI picture: the generator's page showing it, with its prompt, or a screen recording of making it;
  - for a real photo: the camera's original file, or another photo from the same moment.

These never count, because saying so when it can't tell is the whole point:
- **"No answer for this one"**
- **"Can't verify"**
- **"Too little to read"**

Pictures that are partly AI, such as a real photo with AI edits, don't count either way. The honest answer for
them is unclear.

## What happens when you win

- We reply publicly within **48 hours**. We show your picture only if you say we may.
- We describe the kind of picture that fooled it, for example "a portrait re-saved many times". We never name
  the tool that made it or the trick that worked. That would hand a recipe to anyone trying to fool it for
  real.
- You get the credit by your handle, if you want it.
- If we can't agree on whether the proof holds, we say so publicly and explain why.

## And if you can't fool it

If you make videos and Sentinel holds up, we'd love one video about it. That's an ask, not a rule.

## Fair play

- An answer is an estimate, not proof. Never use one to accuse a person of faking a picture.
- Use pictures you have the right to share. No private photos of other people.
- The free check gives 6 checks every 3 hours, and a free account gives 10. Please don't flood it.
- Sentinel keeps no copy of the pictures you check. The receipt is the only record, and it's yours.
